# How to use Clipper Engine

A 5-minute guide from download to your first posted clip. No account or login needed.

---

## Step 1: Install

1. Go to the [download page](https://github.com/rrobin9/clipper-engine#download) and get the file for your computer.
2. **Mac:** unzip it, drag **Clipper Engine** into **Applications** and open it.
   If macOS says it can't check the app, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**,
   scroll down and press **Open Anyway** (once).
3. **Windows:** unzip it (right-click → **Extract All**) and open **Clipper Engine.exe**.
   If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, press **More info → Run anyway** (once).

The app opens in your web browser. In Chrome or Edge, press **Install** in the address bar so it
gets its own window and icon.

> The very first time, the app downloads its captions tool (about 500 MB) in the background.
> You can start clipping right away; captions are added to your clips by themselves when it is done.

---

## Step 2: Find streams (Stream Dashboard)

Open **Stream Dashboard** from the home screen.

- **Live now:** the busiest live GTA streams on Twitch, YouTube and Kick (green mark), ranked by
  how fast their chat is moving.
- **Coming up:** GTA 6 streams that start soon, with a countdown.
- **Pin** a stream to always record it when it's live. **Hide** removes it from the list.
- **Add by name or link** to add your own stream (a Twitch name, a YouTube or Kick link, or an
  @handle).
- **Watch live** (or press **V**) plays the stream in the app with its live chat next to it.
  Under the stream, press **Record**, then **Clip it now** (**C**) or **Mark start → Mark end** (**K**)
  to clip without leaving it. Close it with **Close** or **Esc**.

---

## Step 3: Start clipping (Clipper Engine)

Open **Clipper Engine**. It starts by itself after a short countdown.

- **Auto pilot** (the default) picks the best streams for you. **My streams** records only the
  ones you choose.
- **Stop after** lets you pick how many clips you want (up to 50), or no limit.

Now leave it running. Each stream shows **stars**, its hype meter. When chat explodes, the app
clips that moment **automatically**.

It also clips **game moments**: the WASTED screen or a huge explosion, even when chat is quiet.
Kick streams are clipped this way (the app can't read Kick's chat), so their lanes say
**Game moments**. In the 9:16 version, **reaction zoom** punches in on the streamer's face when
they scream or laugh (switch it off in **Options → Clips**).

Want a clip right now?

- **Clip it now** clips a stream yourself. Choose **Best moment** (it finds the most exciting part
  of the last 3 minutes) or **Last**, and a length (15 s to 2 min).
- **Mark start → Mark end** clips exactly the part between your two presses.

The **Cutting room** on the right shows each clip being made.

---

## Step 4: Wait for it to finish (about a minute)

After a clip is cut, the app adds **captions** and makes the **9:16 vertical** version. This takes
about a minute (longer on slower computers).

You'll see a **progress bar** in the Garage and on the clip itself, for example
*"Making the 9:16 version · 50%"*. You can leave the page while it works. If you close the app,
it finishes the clip the next time you open it.

---

## Step 5: Export and post (GTA 6 Video Garage)

Open **GTA 6 Video Garage**. Every clip is here, newest first.

1. Open a clip. You see both versions: **9:16** (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) and **16:9 widescreen**.
2. Optional: **trim** it, change the **9:16 layout**, or turn captions on or off, then **Re-render**.
3. Copy a **title** and the **hashtags** from the ideas.
4. Press **Export** for **TikTok**, **Instagram Reels**, **YouTube Shorts** or **Landscape**.
5. Press **Show in Finder / Show in Explorer** to find the file, then upload it.

Your clips are saved in **Movies/Clipper Engine** (Mac) or **Videos\Clipper Engine** (Windows).
Exports are in the **_exports** folder inside it.

---

## Handy keys

**Esc** back · **Q** quick menu · **M** sound on/off · **1–9** clip that stream · **K** mark start/end ·
**A** add a stream · **V** watch live with chat

---

## Something not working?

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Export button is greyed out, or "making… 50%" | The clip is still being finished. Wait a minute; it switches on by itself. |
| "The engine is off" | Press **Power on**, or open Clipper Engine again. |
| No clips yet | Keep it running: clips come when chat suddenly speeds up. **Clip it now** always works. |
| A YouTube stream stays "offline" | Turn off your VPN. YouTube sometimes asks VPN or office networks to sign in, and the app doesn't use a login. Twitch streams are not affected. Auto pilot swaps such a stream after 2 minutes. |
| No captions on a clip | The captions tool may still be downloading (first run only), or nobody spoke in the clip. |
| Mac: *"can't be opened (-47)"* | An older version is still running. Press **Power off** in it, then open the new one from Applications. |

**Updating to a new version:** press **Power off** in the app, then replace the app with the new
download (Mac: in Applications; Windows: replace the old unzipped folder). You don't need to uninstall
anything. Your clips and settings stay.

Still stuck? [Open an issue](https://github.com/rrobin9/clipper-engine/issues) and say what you
see and whether you use Mac or Windows.
